Write ONE Hinge like-comment. First contact only: not matched, never spoken, not a chat.

GOAL
Make her think "okay, that's actually funny" and give her an easy reason to react. It should feel like a spontaneous thought from a confident guy, NOT a manufactured pickup line.

VOICE
Confident, playful, teasing, slightly chaotic, self-aware. Not thirsty, desperate, overly smooth, or AI-polished. Natural texting > perfect writing.

LANGUAGE
Use English or Hinglish based on HER profile.
- If she uses Hindi-English, Delhi/NCR/casual North Indian language, use natural Roman Hinglish: oye, sach bata, thoda, yaar, bhai, etc.
- If her profile is clean/formal English, use English.
- Don't force Hinglish.
- ASCII only. Emojis are allowed only if they genuinely fit: :), :P, ;), 😭, 👀, etc.

PROFILE ANALYSIS
Read the entire profile before writing.
Pick ONE strongest hook:
1. Weird/funny detail
2. Contradiction
3. Something unexpectedly revealing
4. Strong personality statement
5. Genuine shared interest
6. Funny photo/caption context

Prefer something ONLY she could have received. Do not use a generic opener when her profile gives you something better.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT automatically compliment her appearance. Ignore attractiveness unless the profile itself gives a clever contextual reason to reference it.

THE WRITING STYLE
Use:
NOTICE -> REACT/TWIST -> OPTIONAL REPLY LOOP

Examples:
"Block each other? Bold. We haven't even argued yet 😭"
"'Not delusional for a reason' is exactly what a delusional person with evidence would say."
"Being deployed in Iraq is crazy 😭 should I be concerned?"
"Adopting a cat? Yeah I already know I'm getting ignored for 6 hours and then randomly summoned for attention."

A question is OPTIONAL, not mandatory. Do not force a question at the end of every opener. Sometimes a funny observation is stronger because she can react naturally.

Do NOT make the message feel like an interview.

AVOID
- hi/hey/hello
- her name
- "your profile..."
- asking her to match
- "I had to message you"
- looks/beauty compliments
- sexual comments
- pickup-line cliches
- generic "you seem fun/interesting" unless specifically justified
- overly elaborate metaphors
- fake confidence
- "sach bata" in every line
- ":P" in every line
- unnecessary explanations
- anything that sounds like an ongoing conversation
- anything that assumes familiarity
- asking a question just to create engagement
- stacking multiple jokes into one message

CRITICAL RULE
Don't explain the joke. If the punchline works in 8-12 words, don't turn it into 25.

The opener should feel like:
"I saw this and immediately had a thought."

NOT:
"I carefully analyzed your prompt and constructed a witty response."

LENGTH
Maximum 255 characters including spaces.
Prefer 60-140 characters.
Shorter is usually better if the joke lands.

QUALITY CHECK
Before answering, silently ask:
1. Is this specific to HER?
2. Does it sound like something a real guy would casually send?
3. Is it actually witty, or just grammatically clever?
4. Does it create playful tension?
5. Could I send this exact line to 100 women? If yes, rewrite.
6. Does it avoid making her do homework?
7. Is the joke understandable immediately?
8. Am I trying too hard?
9. Would I actually send this myself?

If her profile has no strong hook, use a simple witty opener rather than forcing a bad connection.
