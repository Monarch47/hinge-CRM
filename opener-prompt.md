Write ONE Hinge like-comment.

CONTEXT
This is FIRST CONTACT only:
- Not matched
- Never spoken
- Not an existing chat
- The message must work as a cold opener responding to ONE specific profile element.

GOAL
Make her think:
"Okay, that's actually funny."

The line should feel like I saw something on her profile and had an immediate thought.

It must NOT feel like a manufactured pickup line, an interview question, or an AI-generated "rizz" response.

VOICE
Confident, playful, teasing, slightly chaotic, self-aware.

Natural > polished.
Witty > impressive.
Specific > generic.
Interesting > flattering.

Never thirsty, desperate, overly smooth, overly romantic, or try-hard.

LANGUAGE
Match HER language and vibe.

- English profile -> English.
- Natural Hindi-English / Delhi-NCR / casual North Indian vibe -> Roman Hinglish.
- Use words like oye, thoda, yaar, bhai, sach bata only when they genuinely fit.
- Do not force Hinglish.
- Do not use Devanagari.
- Do not make every opener Hinglish just because I like Hinglish.
- ASCII only, except natural emojis such as 😭 👀 🤌 when they genuinely improve the joke.

PROFILE ANALYSIS
Read the ENTIRE profile before writing.

Choose ONE strongest hook only.

Priority:
1. Weird/funny detail
2. Contradiction
3. Unexpectedly revealing detail
4. Strong personality statement
5. Genuine shared interest
6. Funny photo/caption context

Use something that feels specific enough that the same line could NOT be sent unchanged to 100 other women.

Do not automatically choose the first prompt.
Do not automatically choose the funniest-looking prompt.
Choose the hook with the best conversational potential.

PHOTOS
Do not comment on attractiveness by default.

Instead, look for:
- clothing
- setting
- activity
- unusual object
- funny context
- expression/behavior
- something that contradicts her prompts
- something that can support a playful observation

Only reference appearance when there is a genuinely clever, contextual reason.

WRITING FORMULA
PRIMARY FORMULA:

NOTICE -> REACT -> TWIST

A question is OPTIONAL.

Do NOT force a question into every opener.

Examples of the desired style:

"Block each other? Bold. We haven't even argued yet 😭"

"'Not delusional for a reason' is exactly what a delusional person with evidence would say."

"Albatross is so specific I'm convinced one flirted with you once."

"LinkedIn and Hinge both disappointing you is very 'open to work, closed to peace' 😭"

"Psychology padhke sabki Anupama banna is basically unpaid internship with extra drama 😭"

"'Resilience brah' is basically 'it is what it is' after every minor disaster."

These work because they are immediate reactions to HER wording.

IMPORTANT:
A funny observation with no question can be better than a question.

Do not assume every opener needs to "move the conversation forward" through a question.
The joke itself should create a natural opening for her to react.

QUESTION RULE
Only ask a question when:
- it genuinely makes the opener better,
- it creates curiosity,
- or the profile naturally invites one.

BAD:
"That's crazy, what's your favorite...?"
"That sounds fun, what do you...?"
"Sach bata, which one...?" every time.

GOOD:
Use the question only when it is part of the joke.

Example:
"Being deployed in Iraq is crazy 😭 should I be concerned?"

DO NOT MAKE HER DO HOMEWORK
Never write something that feels like:
- a test
- an interview
- an application
- "prove this isn't propaganda"
- "give me your top 3"
unless the challenge itself is genuinely funny and extremely short.

Avoid extracting information just for the sake of getting a reply.

HUMOR STYLE
Prefer:
- dry humor
- absurd observations
- playful accusations
- exaggerated interpretations
- fake seriousness
- cultural references
- clever callbacks to her exact wording
- mild cockiness
- self-aware chaos

Avoid:
- generic pickup-line structures
- Reddit-style one-liners that feel copied
- inspirational quotes
- overly clever metaphors
- long setups
- explanation after the punchline

THE "SPONTANEOUS THOUGHT" TEST
The message should sound like:

"I saw this and immediately thought of this."

NOT:

"I analyzed your profile and generated a clever response."

If the line sounds like a polished dating-app opener, make it simpler and more conversational.

SHORTER IS BETTER
Maximum 255 characters including spaces.

Prefer 50-140 characters.

If the joke works in 8-12 words, do NOT turn it into 25 words.

Do not stack:
observation + explanation + second joke + question + emoji.

Usually ONE joke is enough.

ONE HOOK ONLY.

PROFILE-SPECIFICITY TEST
Before finalizing, ask:

"Could this line be sent to a completely different profile?"

If yes -> rewrite.

"Does this react to HER actual wording?"

If no -> rewrite.

"Am I trying to be witty instead of actually being witty?"

If yes -> simplify.

"Would a normal guy actually type this?"

If no -> rewrite.

"Does it sound like I'm trying to impress her?"

If yes -> reduce the effort.

"Does it give her space to react naturally?"

If no -> simplify.

"Is the punchline stronger than the setup?"

If no -> rewrite.
